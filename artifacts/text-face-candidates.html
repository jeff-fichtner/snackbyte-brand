Two faces on any page: Bricolage Grotesque, and one text face. Inter everywhere. Source Serif 4 for legal and financial paper, where the name is Snackbyte LLC.

Everywhere: Inter
The site's pages and the tools. Bricolage keeps the wordmark and h1. h2 sets in Inter, so two grotesques never meet at similar sizes.
Legal and financial paper: Source Serif 4
Privacy policy, terms, contracts, invoices, receipts: the documents that already carry the legal name. The serif replaces Inter for the whole document, h2 included. Bricolage keeps the wordmark and h1.
Everything else follows the text face
h3, body text, tables, captions, labels and buttons. Two weights, 400 and 600; body text at line height 1.5 in both faces, tables and captions at 14px and 1.45. A document never mixes Inter and the serif.

How the numbers were taken. x-height is the top of the lowercase x over the em, at weight 400, and at optical size 14 where the face has that axis. "In a table" is that x-height at 0.875rem. Set width is the brand's subhead at weight 400 at body size, against Bricolage's text cut.

Also considered and set aside: Newsreader, Instrument Sans, Hanken Grotesk and Schibsted Grotesk. Source Serif 4 is set at line height 1.6 and Inter at 1.5, as each would be in use. All three faces are OFL and load from Google Fonts, as the guide requires. Colors and sizes are the brand's tokens, v1.3.1.
